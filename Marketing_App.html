Leading IT Forward

How Leadership Styles Drive Organizational Success in the Information Age

Edward Daly, DBA
University of Phoenix | Phoenix, Arizona

Why This Book Matters

“Leadership style is the single most consequential variable in whether IT projects, systems, and organizations succeed or fail — yet it remains the most underinvested factor in technology management.”

This landmark study bridges leadership theory and IT practice...

Landmark Research Findings

  • r = .64 — Inspirational motivation strongly predicts customer service outcomes.
  • 157 IT professionals surveyed across public, private, and nonprofit sectors.
  • p < .01 — All transformational factors show statistically significant positive relationships.

The Study

  • MLQ 5x
  • Balanced Scorecard
  • Transformational, Transactional & Laissez‑Faire leadership styles
  • One‑Way ANOVA

Key Findings

  • Transformational leadership drives IT success across all sectors.
  • Inspirational motivation is the strongest predictor.
  • Contingent reward plays a supporting role.
  • Laissez‑faire leadership damages outcomes.
